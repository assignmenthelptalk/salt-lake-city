### Lead and Arsenic Removal

Lead usually enters water from service lines and home plumbing, not from the utility's source. A certified filter or [reverse osmosis system](/reverse-osmosis-installation/) reduces lead and arsenic at the tap. SLCDPU's 2026 report says it has completed an initial lead service line inventory, so check your service line material as well as filtering.

### Chloramine and Chlorine Removal

Chlorine and chloramine are disinfectants, and SLCDPU's report says it applies chlorine or other disinfection methods at its plants. A catalytic carbon filter reduces both, along with the taste and smell they leave. <mark class="data-gap">[NEEDS DATA: whether SLCDPU or your provider uses chloramine.]</mark>

### Fluoride Filter Installation

SLCDPU stopped adding fluoride in May 2025 after a Utah law, and its 2026 report says fluoride is naturally present at 0.0 to 0.28 mg/L with a system-wide average of 0.115 mg/L. A reverse osmosis or specialty media filter reduces fluoride at the tap for homeowners who want it lower.

</div>

### PFAS Water Filter

PFAS are a group of persistent chemicals, and a carbon block or reverse osmosis filter certified for PFAS reduces them at the tap. <mark class="data-gap">[NEEDS DATA: SLCDPU's PFAS results; the 2026 report's data table lists only the substances detected.]</mark>

### Nitrate Filter Installation

Nitrate is a concern mainly for [private wells](/well-water-softener-salt-lake-city-ut/) and for infants, and an ion exchange or reverse osmosis system reduces it. SLCDPU's 2026 report lists nitrate in its table against a 10 ppm limit.

</div>

### VOC (Volatile Organic Compound) Filter

A carbon filter reduces volatile organic compounds, which come from solvents and fuels. A water test shows whether any are present before a filter is chosen.
