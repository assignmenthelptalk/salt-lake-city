## Why Is Salt Lake City Water So Hard?

Salt Lake City water is hard because of where it comes from.

### Where Does Salt Lake City Water Come From?

According to the SLCDPU 2026 Water Quality Report, the city draws water from Wasatch Range streams (City Creek, Parleys Creek, Big Cottonwood Creek and Little Cottonwood Creek) treated at three surface water plants. In summer, as stream runoff falls, SLCDPU adds groundwater from wells. It also buys treated water from the Metropolitan Water District of Salt Lake and Sandy and from the Jordan Valley Water Conservancy District.

</div>

<div class="issue-item">

### How Hard Is Salt Lake City Water?

SLCDPU's report lists total hardness of 136 mg/L (8.0 grains per gallon) at its Big Cottonwood plant, 175 mg/L (10.2 gpg) at Parleys and 212 mg/L (12.4 gpg) at City Creek. SLC wells measured 154 to 571 mg/L, or 9.6 to 33.4 gpg, and Jordan Valley district water measured 184.1 mg/L, about 10.8 gpg. The report gives no single city-wide average, so your street's reading depends on the blend that reaches it.

</div>

<div class="issue-item">

### How Is Water Hardness Measured?

Hardness is measured in milligrams per liter (mg/L) or grains per gallon (gpg), where 1 gpg equals about 17.1 mg/L. The U.S. Geological Survey rates water under 60 mg/L as soft, 61 to 120 mg/L as moderately hard, 121 to 180 mg/L as hard and anything over 180 mg/L as very hard. By that scale, City Creek and Jordan Valley water test very hard and Big Cottonwood water tests hard.

</div>

<div class="issue-item">

### What Does Hard Water Do to a Home?

Hard water leaves marks across the whole house. Scale builds inside water heaters, dishwashers and washing machines, narrows pipes, clogs showerheads and leaves white crust on faucets. Hard water also reacts with soap to form a film that leaves skin dry and hair dull, and minerals stiffen laundry.

</div>

<div class="issue-item">

### How Do You Find Your Exact Hardness?

A free water test shows the exact reading at your tap, because the supply blend and home plumbing both move the number. The technician records it in grains per gallon and sizes the softener to that figure.

</div>

<div class="issue-item">

### How Does a Water Softener Fix Hard Water?

A water softener removes calcium and magnesium through ion exchange and replaces them with sodium or potassium, so scale stops forming and soap lathers properly. For Salt Lake City homeowners, that single change protects the water heater, the plumbing and the fixtures at the same time.
