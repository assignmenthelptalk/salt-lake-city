## What Wears Out in a Water Softener?

Three parts decide the lifespan: the resin, the control head and the tank.

- **Resin:** the beads that exchange hardness wear out and foul with iron, so water turns hard even when the unit runs. A resin bed [replacement](/water-softener-replacement/) restores them.
- **Control head and valve:** seals, the motor and the timer wear with each regeneration, and a failed head can be repaired or replaced.
- **Tank:** a sound tank lasts longest, and a leaking or cracked tank means replacement.

## How Does Salt Lake City Water Affect Softener Life?

Harder water means more regenerations and more capacity used. SLCDPU's wells reach 33.4 grains per gallon, against 8.0 at its Big Cottonwood plant, so a unit on the harder water works through its capacity faster. Iron in well water also fouls resin, so a pre-filter extends the life.

## How to Make a Softener Last

Check the salt monthly, clean the brine tank when salt bridges form and schedule a yearly inspection. For service, see [Water Softener Maintenance in Salt Lake City, UT](/water-softener-maintenance/).
