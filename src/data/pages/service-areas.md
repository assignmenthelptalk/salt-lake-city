### Salt Lake City

Salt Lake City homes receive SLCDPU water, which lists total hardness from 136 mg/L at the Big Cottonwood plant to 212 mg/L at the City Creek plant, and up to 571 mg/L from its wells. A free test shows the reading at your tap.

### Millcreek, Holladay and Cottonwood Heights

SLCDPU's report says its service area includes portions of Millcreek, Holladay and Cottonwood Heights, so some homes there receive the same source blend as Salt Lake City, and others are served by a different provider. Check your water bill for the provider name, and a free test confirms the hardness. Dedicated pages for these three communities follow the launch phase.
