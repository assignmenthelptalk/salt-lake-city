## What Does Water Quality Testing Measure in Salt Lake City, UT?

Seven tests cover hardness, total dissolved solids, iron, pH, chlorine, sulfur and heavy metals.

<div class="svc-grid">

<div class="svc-card">


![Free Hard Water Test Salt Lake City UT](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Free Hard Water Test

A [hard water](/water-softener-guides/hard-water-salt-lake-city-ut/) test reads calcium and magnesium in grains per gallon. SLCDPU lists 8.0 to 12.4 gpg at its surface water plants, and the test shows your street's number so a softener is sized correctly.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![TDS Testing Salt Lake City UT](/placeholders/card.svg)

### TDS Testing

Total dissolved solids measures all dissolved minerals in milligrams per liter. A high reading points to [reverse osmosis](/reverse-osmosis-installation/) or another treatment for drinking water.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Iron Content Testing Salt Lake City UT](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Iron Content Testing

Iron stains fixtures and laundry and fouls softener resin. A reading above the softener's limit means an iron filter should go in first.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![pH Level Testing Salt Lake City UT](/placeholders/card.svg)

### pH Level Testing

pH shows whether water is acidic or alkaline. Low pH corrodes pipes, and the reading guides material and treatment choices.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Chlorine Testing Salt Lake City UT](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Chlorine Testing

Chlorine is the disinfectant in city water, and SLCDPU's report states it adds chlorine or another disinfectant at its plants. A tap test shows the level at your home, which affects taste and odor.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Sulfur Testing Salt Lake City UT](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Sulfur Testing

Sulfur gives a rotten-egg smell, and it is mainly a [private well](/well-water-softener-salt-lake-city-ut/) issue. A positive test points to a hydrogen sulfide filter.

</div>

<div class="svc-card">


![Heavy Metals Testing Salt Lake City UT](/placeholders/card.svg)

### Heavy Metals Testing

A metals panel checks for lead, arsenic and similar [contaminants](/water-contaminant-removal-salt-lake-city-ut/). SLCDPU's 2026 report describes lead service line replacement funding, and a home test shows what reaches your tap through your own plumbing.

</div>

</div>
